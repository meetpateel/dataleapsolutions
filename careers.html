We're Not Hiring Right Now

DataLeap is an early-stage, founder-led company. Client work is led and delivered by founder Meet Patel, and we don't have any open positions at the moment.

When we do open a role, it will be posted on this page first. If you see a DataLeap job listed anywhere else that isn't also listed here, it isn't from us.

Independent Specialists

Occasionally a client project needs extra depth in data engineering, AI, analytics, or data strategy. If you are an experienced independent consultant, you're welcome to introduce yourself below. Meet reads every message, and we'll reach out only if a project is a genuine fit.

Any contract work is agreed in writing, with scope, rate, and timeline, before it starts.

A Note on Recruitment Scams

Fake job offers sometimes use real company names. DataLeap will never ask you to pay a fee, buy equipment, or share bank or ID details as part of a hiring conversation, and we only email from @dataleapsolution.com addresses. If something feels off, email info@dataleapsolution.com to check.
