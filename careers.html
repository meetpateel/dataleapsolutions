Protect Yourself From Recruitment Scams

Fake job offers sometimes use real company names. DataLeap will never:

  • Ask you to pay a fee, buy equipment, or deposit a check.
  • Ask for your bank details, Social Security number, or ID before a contract is signed.
  • Interview you only by text message or chat app.
  • Contact you from any email domain other than @dataleapsolution.com.

If someone claiming to be DataLeap contacts you and something feels off, email info@dataleapsolution.com or call +1 605-370-3803 to verify.
