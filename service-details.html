Operations App Development

Custom web apps for frontline teams, written by us and owned by you. We start from the forms, checklists, and binders your staff already use and turn them into software that keeps a record and checks the work.

  • Shift checklists, logs, work orders, inspections, and handoffs, with the right access for every role.
  • Time clocks, timesheets, and approval workflows with exports to Excel or your payroll provider.
  • Mobile-friendly, so staff can use them on a phone, and support for multiple locations from day one.

Our HotelOps app for a hotel owner-operator is built this way: ASP.NET Core and SQL Server, roles from front desk to general manager, and about 980 automated tests.

Paperwork & Document Automation

Stop retyping what is already on paper. We build tools that scan, read, and file bills, ledgers, statements, and forms, so data is entered once.

  • OCR and AI extraction for PDFs, scans, and email attachments.
  • A person reviews and approves before anything is posted.
  • Every record links back to its source document, so nothing is lost or untraceable.

AI is used only where it saves real time, and the business rules stay in code you can read and test.

Back-Office & Financial Reporting

Turn bills, expenses, payroll hours, and daily revenue reports into reliable financials without the end-of-month spreadsheet scramble.

  • Expense logs, bank and credit-card reconciliation, and tax reconciliation against daily revenue.
  • P&L roll-ups across locations, with every row traceable to its source.
  • KPI dashboards built around the numbers your business actually runs on.

For example, a hotel owner gets cost per occupied room, GOP, and NOI by property, built from the same bills and daily revenue reports the team already produces.

Data Integration

Connect the tools you already have so information flows between them instead of being copied by hand.

  • Integrations with spreadsheets, Google Workspace, Excel, accounting exports, and SQL databases.
  • Scheduled jobs and scripts that move and clean data automatically.
  • Built-in checks that catch errors before they reach a report.

We write the integration code ourselves, so there are no extra platforms to license.

AI Workflow Automation

Practical AI built into everyday work, not a chatbot bolted on the side.

  • Reading documents, summarizing notes, and flagging exceptions for review.
  • People stay in control: AI suggests, your team decides.
  • Clear limits on what data the AI sees, and switches to turn AI features off.

Every AI feature is measured against the manual process it replaces.

Enablement & Support

Support adoption through hands-on training, written runbooks, and a 90-day support period. We do not disappear after go-live.

  • Training tailored to frontline staff, managers, and whoever maintains the system.
  • Written runbooks so your team knows how everything works.
  • A prioritized enhancement backlog based on how the tools are actually used.

Concretely: your team gets 4 hours/week of dedicated support for 90 days, a monthly review, and a prioritized enhancement backlog.
