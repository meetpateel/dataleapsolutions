About DataLeap

Meet Patel

Founder & Principal Consultant

A boutique consultancy built on hands-on expertise

DataLeap is a founder-led software and data consultancy founded by Meet Patel and based in Sioux Falls, South Dakota. We build operations software, paperwork automation, and back-office reporting for businesses that run on forms and spreadsheets, starting with hotels.

Rather than reselling someone else's platform, we write our own software. Meet designs and builds the full stack, from databases and back-end services to the web apps staff use on the floor, plus the scripts and AI tools that read paperwork and keep the books. Clients get software shaped around how they actually work, and they own the code.

As a focused, founder-led firm, DataLeap offers the senior-level attention and accountability that larger consultancies often cannot. Every engagement is led directly by Meet, ensuring hands-on delivery, clear communication, and outcomes aligned to your business goals.

Core Expertise

Custom Software Development

Web apps and back-end services written from scratch, including ASP.NET Core and SQL Server apps with automated test suites.

Paperwork & AI Automation

OCR and AI that read bills, ledgers, and forms, with a person approving the results and every record linked to its source.

Back-Office Reporting

Python and spreadsheet automation that turns bills and daily reports into reconciled P&Ls and KPI dashboards.

Change Enablement

Hands-on training workshops, runbook documentation, and a 90-day post-launch support period with weekly office hours so your team operates independently.
