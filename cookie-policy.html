Cookie Policy

Effective Date: October 11, 2026

Last Updated: October 11, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Data Leap Solutions Inc ("DataLeap," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on this website. Read it together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, which lets a site remember a setting on your device.

2. What We Use

We do not use analytics, advertising, social media, or other tracking cookies. We use only the following:

Name Provider Purpose Type and duration When it is set
dataleap-cookie-consent DataLeap Remembers whether you accepted or declined reCAPTCHA so we don't ask again on every page. Local storage, kept until you clear your browser data When you click a button in the cookie banner (strictly necessary)
_GRECAPTCHA and related Google cookies Google LLC (reCAPTCHA v3) Spam and abuse protection for our contact and career forms. Google analyzes device and interaction data to tell humans from bots. Third-party cookie, up to 6 months Only if you click "Accept reCAPTCHA", and only on pages with a form

Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Our website host, GitHub Pages, does not set cookies on this site.

3. Your Choices

When you first visit, a banner asks whether to allow reCAPTCHA. "Accept" and "Decline" are equally easy, and declining does not limit your use of the website. If you decline, our forms will not submit, but you can still reach us at info@dataleapsolution.com or +1 605-370-3803.

You can change your choice at any time:

You can also delete cookies or block them in your browser settings. If you withdraw consent, Google cookies that were already set stay in your browser until they expire or you delete them.

4. Changes to This Policy

If we add or change cookies, we will update this page and its "Last Updated" date, and ask for your consent again where required.
