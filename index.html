HotelOps: one app for the front desk, housekeeping, maintenance, and managers.

Built for a hotel owner-operator to replace the binders and paper logs that every shift fills in by hand.

  • Shift checklists for front desk and housekeeping, imported from the hotel's existing Word and Excel forms and verified by a manager.
  • Shift logs, work orders, inspections, lost and found, and housekeeping room boards, all with a full audit trail.
  • Night-audit ledger scanning that flags over-limit guest accounts for a manager to review.
  • Time clock, timesheets, and payroll approval, with pay-period reports exported to Excel.

Supports multiple hotels and management companies, each with its own private data, and installs on staff phones like an app. Built on ASP.NET Core and SQL Server and covered by about 980 automated tests.

Back-office automation: from a stack of bills to a monthly P&L.

Built for a hotel owner-operator with three select-service hotels in South Dakota and Minnesota.

  • Bills are filed by property, read with OCR and AI, and booked to an expense log, with the owner reviewing each one.
  • Expenses roll up into a portfolio P&L using USALI, the hotel industry's accounting standard, with every row linked to its source document.
  • Lodging and sales tax reconciled against daily revenue reports, plus bank and credit-card reconciliation.
  • KPI dashboards for cost per occupied room, GOP, and NOI. Adding another hotel takes one new row.

Runs on Python and Google Workspace, in use at the first property and rolling out to the others.
